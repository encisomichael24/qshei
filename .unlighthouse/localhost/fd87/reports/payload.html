Why Patients trust us

1. Specialized, Expert Care

You are in highly qualified hands. As an AHPRA-registered Nurse Practitioner and Clinical Sexologist, Linda brings specialized, advanced knowledge spanning sexual health, forensic medicine, and emergency care. You receive expert guidance grounded in extensive, real-world clinical experience.

2. A Safe, Sex-Positive Space

Your comfort and peace of mind are the top priorities. QSHEI is built on providing an inclusive, sex-positive, and completely non-judgmental environment. Whatever your health journey looks like, you will always be met with empathy, respect, and absolute confidentiality.

3. Dedicated, One-on-One Attention

At QSHEI, you aren't just a file passed between different doctors in a busy clinic. Because you work directly with Linda every single time, you get true continuity of care. Your history is understood, your appointments are deeply personalized, and your treatment is handled with the undivided attention you deserve.

Our Services

Comprehensive, evidence-based care in a safe and supportive environment.

Sexual & Reproductive Healthcare

From routine health checks to managing life transitions, you can access expert, evidence-based care tailored to your body.

  • Comprehensive STI testing and treatment
  • Contraceptive counselling and procedures, including IUD and implanon insertions
  • Menstrual health management
  • Peri-menopause and menopause care

Clinical Sexology & Pelvic Pain Management

If you are experiencing discomfort or navigating complex sexual health concerns, Linda provides specialised, deeply empathetic support.

Her clinical sexology services focus on:

  • Management of endometriosis and pelvic pain
  • Treatment for painful sex
  • Holistic support integrating the fundamental principles of sexology to improve your overall sexual wellbeing and comfort

Pregnancy Options & Support

Navigating pregnancy decisions requires a supportive, strictly confidential space. QSHEI is here to help you understand your choices so you can make the decision that is best for you.

  • Compassionate pregnancy options support
  • Medical abortion services (no referral necessary)

Inclusive Education & Consulting

Beyond the clinic, QSHEI is committed to fostering safe, informed communities.

Linda brings her expertise to schools, workplaces, and community groups, offering:

  • Respectful relationships and consent education
  • Customised workshops designed to promote inclusivity, trauma-informed practices, and sexual health awareness
